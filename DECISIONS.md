- 2026-07-28: **Ephemeris-dependent test infra: a scoped `fetch` polyfill for `file://` URLs (`tests/helpers/wasmFetchPolyfill.ts`), wired in via `vitest.config.ts` `setupFiles`.** `@swisseph/browser`'s WASM loader is fetch()-based with no Node-native file-read path; its default `init()` resolves the wasm binary to a `file://` URL, which Node's built-in fetch (undici) can't read. Applied globally via `setupFiles` since it's a no-op for the 5 pure-logic test suites.

- 2026-07-28: **Refined the pre-1906 India timezone handling further: Bombay and Calcutta specifically kept their own official local civil time well past 1906.** Prompted by an optional ask to spot-check the pre-1906 LMT logic against a real published value -- found more than a citation, found a real gap. Corroboration, not just trust in one source: Calcutta's +05:53:20 figure matches IANA tzdata's independently documented "HMT" value to the second (see the entry above), and both fixed offsets are within a minute of straightforward longitude-based LMT for each city's actual coordinates. Confidence caveat: Wikipedia is a tertiary source here and no more precise day/month for either cutover was found, only the years -- flagged in the code's own caveat message. Implementation (`src/engine/timezone.ts`): added an `INDIA_EXTENDED_LOCAL_TIME` table keyed by GeoNames geonameId (Mumbai 1275339, Kolkata 1275004) so this only fires for births specifically geocoded to those two cities, not all of India -- `geocodePlace()` (`geocoding.ts`) now exposes `geonameId` on its result, threaded through `resolveLocation()` (`location.ts`) into `resolveUtcOffset()`. A Bombay or Calcutta birth between 1906 and their respective cutover now gets the documented fixed offset (taking precedence over both the generic pre-1906 longitude estimate and national IST) instead of silently defaulting to national IST for that whole window, which would have been wrong by 39 minutes (Bombay) or 23 minutes 20 seconds (Calcutta) -- large enough to matter for Ascendant-sensitive claims. Tests added to `tests/timezone.test.ts` and `tests/location.test.ts` covering both cities' extended windows, the switch back to national IST at each cutover, and confirming a third Indian city (Chennai) is unaffected.

- 2026-07-28: **Bug found and fixed while wiring this: a 1ms floating-point rounding error in `julianDayUTToUtcISO()`.** Subtracting two ~2.4-million-magnitude floats (`julianDayUT - JULIAN_DAY_UNIX_EPOCH`) and scaling by 86.4M left ~0.01ms of floating-point noise, which for an exact birth time (e.g. 15:30:00.000) rounded DOWN across a whole-minute boundary (09:59:59.999Z instead of 10:00:00.000Z), silently reconstituting as 15:29 instead of 15:30 one layer up.
